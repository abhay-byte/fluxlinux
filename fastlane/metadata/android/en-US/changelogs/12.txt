FluxLinux v2.0.0

* Embedded host terminal and embedded X11 server — no external Termux APK required
* Multi-distro catalog: Debian, Alpine, Fedora, Void, openSUSE, Deepin, Chimera, Manjaro, Ubuntu, Kali, Parrot, and Arch Linux
* On-demand rootfs downloads with SHA256 integrity verification
* Integrated PulseAudio audio server and hardware acceleration (Turnip/VirGL)
* Unified PRoot and Chroot environment management
